Hi everyone,  

It’s been a little while since the last update (back in March!), and I’m excited to share some of the things that have been growing in the garden since then. I took a bit of a break earlier this year, but over the summer I dove back in and made some pretty big changes.  

## 📝 Summarization & Fact Extraction  

This is one of the biggest updates yet. I’ve built a new tool that:  

1. Reads through your cards and extracts what it thinks are the **main theses, arguments, and supporting facts**  
2. Generates a clean **summary** for you to read  
3. Saves the extracted facts so you can **explore and connect them** through entities  

What you end up with is like having a **shadow zettelkasten** built alongside your garden. It works far better for summarization than anything else I’ve tried — and it automatically gives you a pre-linked web of information.  

This feature is available in the **Zettelgarden Pro** tier (it’s a bit costly to run), but if you’d like to try it out, just let me know and I’ll happily set you up with a free trial.  

## 🔍 Search is Better (and Faster!)  

I’ve switched Zettelgarden search over to [Typesense](https://typesense.org/). The result: search is now faster, works more smoothly, and feels a lot more reliable.  

I also wrote about the process and lessons learned if you want to dig in: [What I Learned Rebuilding Search](https://nsavage.substack.com/p/what-i-learned-rebuilding-search?r=rj3uw).  

## 🌟 Other Improvements  

- **Performance upgrades**: Zettelgarden now transfers significantly less data, so things should feel much snappier.  
- **GitHub login**: Easier sign-up and login for developers (or anyone with a GitHub account).  
- Lots of smaller quality-of-life improvements across the board.
